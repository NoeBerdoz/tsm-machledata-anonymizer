The leak target passed on both captured boundaries. The restoration target failed in the French request. GLiNER2-PII tagged the literal `<PERSON_1>` in `suivi.txt` as an account ID. The app substituted `<ACCOUNT_ID_1>`, which its restoration code left on screen.
